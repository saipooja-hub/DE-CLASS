OPERATORS IN PYTHON:
These perform common mathematical operations. 
+: Addition (e.g., x + y)
-: Subtraction (e.g., x - y)
*: Multiplication (e.g., x * y)
/: Division (always returns a float, e.g., x / y)
%: Modulus (returns the remainder of a division, e.g., x % y)
**: Exponentiation (raises the first operand to the power of the second, e.g., x ** y)
//: Floor division (returns the quotient as an integer)
a = 10
b = 5
print(a+b)
print(a-b)
print(a*b)
print(a/b)
print(a%b)
print(a**b)
print(a//b)
